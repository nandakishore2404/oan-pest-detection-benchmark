🏆 Section 35: Final Decision Summary

Recommended Disease Classifier: MobileNetV4 Conv Small (87.95% test accuracy, 9.51 MB ONNX, 1.99 ms CPU latency). Permissive Apache-2.0 license.

Recommended Insect Detector: Ultralytics YOLOv8s / YOLO11s (22.3% mAP50 on 12-class pipeline, 11.6 MB ONNX, 15.5 ms CPU latency). Bounding-box counting is mandatory for spray thresholds.

The Kenyan Taxonomy Gap: Exactly 0 labeled detector bounding boxes exist today for the 15 real Kenyan economic taxa. Sourcing via the 3 photos/officer/week pilot (50,400 clean images) is the primary path forward.
